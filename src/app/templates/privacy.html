Plain-language project note

Privacy, for this demo.

This is a local student prototype, not an operating store.

What the site sends locally

Product search terms and shop-guide messages are sent to the FastAPI service running on your computer at 127.0.0.1. The current app does not save them to a database. The development server may print request URLs, including product search terms, in its terminal access log; use synthetic text only.

What stays in your browser

The demo bag stores synthetic product IDs and quantities in this browser’s local storage so it survives a refresh. You can clear it from the bag page or clear this site’s browser storage.

Contact and checkout

The contact form clears after its local preview and does not send or persist the entered text. Checkout has no address or payment fields and does not create a real order.

Use synthetic details only

Do not enter real names, email addresses, order information, payment details, or other personal data. The catalog data is synthetic and the shop guide is still a placeholder.
