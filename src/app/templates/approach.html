A note on how we choose

Fewer things.
Better considered.

We like objects that do their job quietly, feel good in the hand, and stick around longer than a season.

M / 01
01

Useful first

Every piece should solve an everyday problem. The catalog is small on purpose: a bag for going places, a mug for taking a pause, a lamp for settling in.

02

Honest details

Clear descriptions and visible prices make it easier to decide. In this project, every product and product detail is synthetic and used for demonstration.

03

Keep it simple

Search, compare, and add items to a local demo bag. No account, payment details, or real order are needed to explore the site.

A project, in progress

Morrow Supply is a fictional shop built to explore a safer shopping assistant.

The assistant, authorization model, and model-generated descriptions will be developed as separate project stages.
